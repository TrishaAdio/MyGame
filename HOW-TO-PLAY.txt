Castle of Magic PC

EASIEST: download CastleOfMagic-Setup.exe and double-click it.
It puts a "Castle of Magic" icon on your desktop.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
To remove the game: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Castle of Magic > Uninstall.

Without installing: download CastleOfMagic-PC.zip, extract it, double-click CastleOfMagic.exe.

Personal use only.
